GASTRO-X

Can the senses teach artificial intelligence to suggest meals we would trust, and waste less doing it?

✕

Households produce more than half of the food wasted in the European Union. Generative AI can already suggest what to cook with what is left in the fridge, but many people do not trust a machine on matters of taste.

What we hear, see and feel changes what we taste: the louder the crunch, the fresher a crisp seems. GASTRO-X builds cues like these into AI recipe suggestions, in the words, the image of the dish and the sound of it cooking, and tests whether people then trust them more and throw away less.

The project, in four courses

I.Benchmark2026–27

AI models and home cooks, twenty realistic fridges

II.The senses2027

A gastrophysics handbook for prompting AI

III.The nudge2027–28

Trust, tested in four European countries

IV.The demo2028

An open kitchen assistant, served on this site
